How an X-ray works.

Four steps, from the machine to the image your doctor reads.

  • The tube makes the beam.A controlled burst of X-rays leaves the tube.
  • The beam crosses the body.Dense bone blocks more of it than soft tissue.
  • The detector reads what gets through.It turns the pattern into a digital signal.
  • The console shows the image.The radiographer checks it and sends it to the doctor.
