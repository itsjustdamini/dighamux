Built by an engineer who started at ten.

Engr. Ubah Chuma, from Nnobi, Anambra, founded Digha Mux Limited. This is how he got here.

The road to Digha.

  1. Primary 4

    He wired his first electrical circuits.

  2. Primary 6

    He repaired transistor radios for neighbours.

  3. JSS 2

    A home workshop: he extracted hydrogen from water and built fridges and musical instruments.

  4. JSS 3

    He built a working FM radio station from scratch, Nnobi Radio Station. He showed it at Children's Day in Ogidi, and a signal from Enugu reached Nnobi.

  5. Secondary school

    The Anambra State Government awarded him a scholarship to GTC Enugu, where he scored 97%, the highest score in the school's history.

  6. University years

    He helped final-year UNN students with their projects, then worked and studied at the UNN Equipment Maintenance and Development Centre, researching hospital equipment.

  7. A hard choice

    He declined the Vice Chancellor's letters inviting him back, and moved to Awka to build his own company.

  8. Digha SAT

    He ran Digha SAT for four years, and designed a magnetizing machine at the Enugu Research Centre.

  9. 2012

    Digha Biomedical was established, supplying, repairing and refurbishing hospital equipment.

  10. Giving back

    He donated an ultrasound machine to General Hospital Nnobi, Idemili South.
